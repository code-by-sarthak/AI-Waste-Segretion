Golden Segregation Rules (Do's & Don'ts)

✅ DO's / क्या करें
  • Rinse milk pouches and food plastic containers before dropping into the dry waste bin.
  • Wrap sanitary pads & diapers in paper marked with a red cross or dot.
  • Sell clean paper, cardboard, and metals to your local scrap dealer (Kabadiwala).
  • Compost fruit/vegetable peels at home to create organic fertilizer for house plants.
❌ DON'Ts / क्या न करें
  • Never tie wet kitchen garbage in single-use plastic carry bags.
  • Never flush diapers, sanitary napkins, or wipes into toilets.
  • Never dump toxic batteries or tube lights with regular kitchen trash.
  • Never burn plastic, rubber, or tangled e-waste wires openly.

🇮🇳 Towards Garbage-Free Cities

The Swachhta Revolution: Segregation at Source

01

The Challenge

Indian cities generate over 150,000 tonnes of municipal solid waste daily. When wet and dry waste mix, organic decomposition generates toxic methane gas, foul fires, and leachate that pollutes underground water tables.

02

The AI Solution

EcoSort AI removes ambiguity at the kitchen counter. Instant vision models identify composite packaging, tell citizens the exact bin colour, and protect sanitation workers from dangerous biohazards and broken glass.

03

The Circular Economy

Segregated dry waste flows directly to Kabadiwalas and authorized recycling plants. Organic waste becomes nutrient-dense compost for agriculture. Together, we move towards a zero-landfill India.
